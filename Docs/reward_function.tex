\section*{Reward function}

\begin{equation}
r_t = -\sum_{k=1}^{5} \left( w_1 x_{1,k} + w_2 x_{2,k} + w_3 x_{3,k} + w_4 \left(1 - \cos \psi_t\right) + w_5 \right)
\end{equation}

\noindent where $k$ runs over the $5$ simulated seconds of decision step $t$, and:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $x_{1,k}$ is the number of aircraft within $5$~NM in second $k$ (usually $0$ or $1$), with $w_1 = 100$;
    \item $x_{2,k} \in \{0,1\}$ is $1$ if the aircraft is inside a restricted area in second $k$, with $w_2 = 10$;
    \item $x_{3,k} \in \{0,1\}$ is $1$ if the aircraft is outside the sector in second $k$, with $w_3 = 10$;
    \item $\psi_t$ is the angle between the aircraft's heading and the bearing to its waypoint at the end of the step, with $w_4 = 1$;
    \item $w_5 = 0.1$ is a time penalty per second.
\end{itemize}

\noindent So five seconds in a loss of separation cost $500$ and five seconds in a restricted area $50$. The term $1 - \cos \psi_t$ is $0$ when flying straight at the waypoint and $2$ when flying away from it. If the waypoint is reached during a step, the sum runs only over the seconds flown. The reward is never positive.
