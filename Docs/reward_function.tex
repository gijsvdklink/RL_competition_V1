\section*{Reward function}

\begin{equation}
r_t = -\left( w_1 x_{1,t} + w_2 x_{2,t} + w_3 x_{3,t} + w_4 \left(1 - \cos \psi_t\right) + w_5 x_{5,t} \right)
\end{equation}

\noindent where, for decision step $t$ ($5$~s):
\begin{itemize}
    \item $x_{1,t} \in \{0,1\}$ is $1$ if the aircraft has a loss of separation ($< 5$~NM), with $w_1 = 100$;
    \item $x_{2,t} \in \{0,1\}$ is $1$ if the aircraft is inside a restricted area, with $w_2 = 10$;
    \item $x_{3,t} \in \{0,1\}$ is $1$ if the aircraft is outside the sector, with $w_3 = 10$;
    \item $\psi_t$ is the angle between the aircraft's heading and the bearing to its waypoint, with $w_4 = 1$;
    \item $x_{5,t} = 1$ is a time penalty for every decision step, with $w_5 = 0.1$.
\end{itemize}

\noindent The term $1 - \cos \psi_t$ is $0$ when flying straight at the waypoint and $2$ when flying away from it. The time penalty makes a faster flight to the waypoint better. The reward is never positive.
